The Expert Engagement Gap

If you've consulted in MedTech or Pharma you know the shape of the problem. Every new client repeats the same cycle:

  • 3–6 months of vendor qualification, supplier questionnaires, and management sign-off before the first piece of work.
  • Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), data processing agreements (DPAs), and confidentiality agreements negotiated bilaterally for each engagement.
  • Quality Management System (QMS) onboarding and company-specific training, often duplicated across overlapping standards you already know cold.
  • Document exchange via email, ad-hoc shared drives, or whatever tool the client happens to use. No audit trail, no version control, unclear retention.

The result is unbillable time per client, an unpredictable pipeline, and admin overhead that scales worse than the work. PraxisVerify fixes the engagement layer so you can do the work you're qualified for.

Insurance & Liability

PraxisVerify carries platform-level professional indemnity (PI) cover. You carry your own PI as well. Proof of cover is part of credentialing.

Why both? Platform-level cover sits behind the system: matching engine, secure environment, audit trail. Your PI sits behind your professional judgement on the assignment. Two policies covering two different things.

Cyber. Documents inside the secure environment are covered by the platform's cover for data-handling cyber risk. You don't need a cyber endorsement on your own PI to work on the platform. Your PI covers your professional judgement; ours covers the system.

Does my existing policy cover this? Most MedTech consultant PI policies do. Wording varies between insurers, so check yours. We'll flag any gaps at credentialing rather than assume cover is in place.

Standards. You own and access the published standards your declared specialism is built on (ISO 13485, IEC 60601 series, relevant FDA guidances). Standard ownership is a professional baseline, not reimbursed per assignment. If a one-off needs a specialist standard you don't hold, flag it before accepting and we route it differently.

Data Security & Confidentiality

The work and the documents you handle are sensitive. Here's who can see what, and for how long.

  • Access controls. A client document is visible only to the client who uploaded it and the expert (or experts) assigned to that specific review. No general "browse" access for credentialed experts. Matching is per-assignment.
  • In-browser only. Documents are reviewed inside the platform's secure viewer. No downloads, no local copies, no email attachments. Every action (open, view, annotate, comment) is time-stamped, supporting 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records.
  • Retention and deletion. Client documents and review work product are auto-deleted 90 days after the end of the project. Long or slow-moving projects don't trigger early deletion. The clock starts at project close, not at upload. If a client needs longer retention for a specific submission, an extension is requested in writing before the deletion window.
  • Sub-processors. The current list of platform sub-processors and what they process is published in our Data Processing Agreement (DPA), referenced from the Privacy Policy. Changes are notified to clients in advance.
  • Information security posture. Built to ISO/IEC 27001 control families. The ISMS is operating toward formal certification. Active controls (access management, encryption, audit logging, vulnerability management) are in production and reviewable on request.
  • AI use. Detailed guidance is delivered at onboarding. Two rules to know up front: client documents stay inside the secure environment (no downloads, no copy-paste, no exports). Your own use of AI to support your professional reasoning is your judgement to make. Client-identifying data must never enter a public AI tool.

Vetting Process

This is a selective network, not a gig marketplace. The full process:

  1. Apply. Submit credentials, specialisms, references, and proof of PI insurance.
  2. Credential verification. We verify against the issuing bodies (IRCA, RAPS, CQI, Notified Body alumni networks, and equivalents).
  3. Platform onboarding. Platform NDA, data-handling orientation, in-browser review training. (Client-specific QMS training is automatic per assignment, before document access, not a separate step here.)
  4. First match. You appear in the active matching pool for assignments fitting your specialisms.

Credentialing & invoicing. Credentialing is at individual level. Credential, NDA, audit trail, and PI cover are tied to the named expert who accepted the match. Invoicing can route through your practice's company if that's how you operate. The commercial counterparty is whichever entity you nominate. The regulated counterparty is you. Assignments are non-transferable; if you'd want a named associate in the network, have them apply directly.

FAQ

Is there a minimum number of assignments per month?

No. There is no floor. You stay in the active network as long as your credentials are current.

How quickly will I be matched after credentialing?

Match velocity depends on demand for your declared specialisms. We're transparent about pipeline depth at credentialing. If your specialism is currently low-demand, you'll know.

What happens if a client disputes my assessment?

Your annotations, review comments, and any add-on sign-off are preserved in the audit trail. Disputes go through a documented review process; you retain the right to defend your professional opinion. Regulatory submissions remain the sponsor's responsibility. Your role is qualified independent verification, not authorship.

Can I see what an assignment looks like before accepting?

Yes. You see scope, document type, estimated effort, calculated rate, and any add-on fee triggers before you accept. You can decline without penalty. You should, if your expertise isn't a clean fit. We'd rather reassign than carry a marginal match.

Can I submit a standalone gap document or report instead of inline annotations?

Inline annotation is the primary output. It builds the per-finding audit trail clients and Notified Bodies expect. A structured report can be attached alongside the annotations, but not instead of them. Standalone free-form reports are out of scope at launch.

Where can experts be based at launch?

Ireland on day one. Geographic expansion will follow client demand in other jurisdictions.

What about IP and confidentiality?

Document IP remains the client's. Your assessment work product is governed by the platform NDA. No client documents leave the secure environment. Retention and deletion rules are set out in the Data Security & Confidentiality section above. Documents are auto-deleted 90 days after the end of the project, not 90 days after upload.
